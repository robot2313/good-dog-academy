# Good Dog Academy Support

Good Dog Academy provides practical, reward-based dog-training guidance and stores the current version's training data locally on your device.

## Contact

For app support, bug reports, privacy questions, or help with your data controls, contact:

**GoodDogAcademy1@gmail.com**

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, Android or iOS version, the Good Dog Academy app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive personal information or private dog photos unless specifically necessary for the support request.

## Data and privacy

Good Dog Academy currently has no user account, cloud sync, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. Profile details, training progress, lesson history, assessment answers, Troubleshooter outcomes, settings, and an optional dog photo are stored locally on the device.

To delete app data, open **Dog > Privacy and Your Data > Delete All App Data** and confirm deletion.

See `docs/privacy-policy.md` for the full privacy policy.

## Training and safety

Good Dog Academy provides educational dog-training information and is not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis or individual advice from a qualified reward-based training professional. If a dog may be in pain, is showing sudden behaviour changes, or presents a safety risk, stop the exercise and seek appropriate professional help.
