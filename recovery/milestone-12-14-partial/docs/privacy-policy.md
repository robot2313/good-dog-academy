# Good Dog Academy Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 4 August 2026**  
**Last updated: 4 August 2026**

Good Dog Academy is a dog-training application designed to keep your training information on your own device. This policy explains what information the current version of Good Dog Academy uses, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

## Information used by the app

You may choose to enter or create the following information in Good Dog Academy:

- your name;
- your dog's profile details, including name, birthday or estimated age;
- an optional dog profile photo selected from your device;
- behaviour-assessment answers and generated training recommendations;
- lesson plans, lesson progress, training sessions, achievements, and app settings; and
- Training Troubleshooter answers and saved outcomes.

This information is used only to provide the app's local personalisation, training, progress, history, and troubleshooting features.

## Local storage and data collection

The current version of Good Dog Academy has no Good Dog Academy online account, backend server, cloud synchronisation, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. The information listed above is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device and is not uploaded to or collected by Good Dog Academy.

If you select a dog photo, the app asks for permission to access the photo you choose and copies that photo into Good Dog Academy's private app-managed storage on your device. The app does not request microphone access.

Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, or your operating-system provider may separately process information when you download or use an app, according to their own terms and privacy policies. Good Dog Academy does not control those independent services.

## Sharing

Good Dog Academy does not sell, rent, or share the locally stored information described in this policy with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The current version contains no feature that sends this information to Good Dog Academy.

## Retention and deletion

Your information remains in the app's private storage until you change or delete it, use **Delete All App Data**, or remove the app and its associated data through your device.

To delete the information saved by Good Dog Academy:

1. Open the **Dog** tab.
2. Select **Privacy and Your Data**.
3. Select **Delete All App Data**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

This deletes the owner and dog profile, optional app-managed dog photo, assessment answers, lesson progress, plans, training sessions, achievements, settings, and Training Troubleshooter outcomes saved by the app on that device. The action is permanent. Because the current version does not create an online account or upload this information to Good Dog Academy, there is no server-side Good Dog Academy account or cloud record to delete.

## Children

Good Dog Academy is intended for dog owners and handlers. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any user because it does not send locally entered information to Good Dog Academy. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of the app and can delete all locally stored app information at any time using the steps above.

## Security

Good Dog Academy limits the information it handles by keeping the current app experience local to your device. Your device's security, backups, access controls, and operating-system protections also affect information stored on that device. No storage or security method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised effective or last-updated date. If a future version adds accounts, cloud services, analytics, advertising, or another material data practice, this policy and the app's store disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.
